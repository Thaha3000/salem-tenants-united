Our Community, Our Voice

Salem Tenants United Essex County is a space for residents to stay informed, share ideas, discover community activities, and connect with one another.

Our Mission

To educate, inform, empower, and advocate for fair laws and regulations that support clean, safe, and secure housing and shelter.

Through written, verbal, and visual communication, we strive to connect with our members and interested community members, share important information, and deliver messages that promote awareness, understanding, and positive change.
